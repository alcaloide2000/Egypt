What the axis leaves out

Four sites carry a second date that has nothing to do with when they were built — when the modern world found or moved them. Too recent to plot on a BC axis, so they're listed here instead.

  • 1859 Palermo Stone surfaces in a private purchase in Sicily — its Egyptian findspot never recorded.
  • 1897–98 Narmer Palette excavated at Hierakonpolis, in the Main Deposit of the Temple of Horus.
  • 1968 Abu Simbel's temples cut into 1,000+ blocks and rebuilt on higher ground, escaping Lake Nasser.
  • 1977–80 Philae's Temple of Isis dismantled and moved to Agilkia Island, escaping the Aswan High Dam.

Reading the cluster

Nearly everything between Djoser and Khufu happens inside 110 years. Saqqara's Step Pyramid, the small pyramid on Elephantine, Meidum, both pyramids at Dahshur, and the Great Pyramid itself — five separate building projects, one long apprenticeship in getting a pyramid's angle right, ending at Giza.
