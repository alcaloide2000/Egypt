19–24 November 2026 · Aswan → Luxor

Private Alimaña Tour

Six days on the Nile with a Spanish-speaking Egyptologist: three nights aboard the dahabeya C La Vie 1, plus private days at Abu Simbel and at Dendera & Abydos.

  • Abu Simbel · Philae · Kom Ombo
  • Edfu · Luxor · Dendera & Abydos

Day 1 · Thu 19 Nov

Arrival in Aswan · Abu Simbel

Welcome at about 9:00 AM, then south by private car to the temples Ramesses II cut into the cliff, c. 1264 BC. Night in Aswan.

  • Great Temple of Ramesses II
  • Small temple of Nefertari

Day 2 · Fri 20 Nov

Philae · All aboard

By boat to the Temple of Isis on Agilkia Island, then lunch on the dahabeya as she casts off and sails north.

  • Temple of Isis, Ptolemaic
  • Dahabeya night 1 · full board

Day 3 · Sat 21 Nov

Kom Ombo

Breakfast under sail, then a temple split exactly in two: one half for the crocodile god Sobek, the other for the falcon god Haroeris.

  • Ptolemaic double temple
  • Dahabeya night 2 · full board

Day 4 · Sun 22 Nov

Edfu · Temple of Horus

Built c. 237–57 BC and still roofed: one of the best-preserved temples in Egypt. Then on downriver, past ancient Nekhen.

  • Granite falcons at the gate
  • Dahabeya night 3 · full board

Day 5 · Mon 23 Nov

Luxor · Thebes, both banks

Off the boat after breakfast for a full day in ancient Thebes, then on to the Luxor hotel.

  • Valley of the Kings
  • Temple of Hatshepsut · Colossi of Memnon
  • Karnak Temple

Day 6 · Tue 24 Nov

Dendera & Abydos

A private day trip north: Hathor's temple, roof and zodiac ceiling intact, then Seti I's temple with its list of 76 kings. Back in Luxor around 5:00 PM.

  • Temple of Hathor, Dendera
  • Temple of Seti I, Abydos
